## What I Built
Trailhead tells you what to do outside in the time you actually have, before the light runs out. The screen is the shortest part: one command, one short plan, phone away.

## How I Built It
Python stdlib + a local open-weight model through Ollama. Sunset and frost countdowns are computed offline; the model only phrases the plan from those facts.

## Why Does Open Innovation Matter?
It works on a trailhead with no signal, nothing about where you go leaves your device, it costs nothing to run, and you can swap the model with one flag.
